# Media Assets

Photographs, schematics, and video demonstrations of the ESP8266 Servo-Based Hand Assistance prototype:

- **`prototype.jpg`**: Primary hardware prototype setup featuring the ESP8266 NodeMCU V3, custom perfboard power bus, and 5 SG90 mini servo actuators.
- **`prototype_view2.jpg`**: Alternate angle photograph of the prototype breadboard/perfboard assembly and cabling.
- **`demonstration.mp4`**: Video demonstration showing servo sweep and hold cycles (to be added).
